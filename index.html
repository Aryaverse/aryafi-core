CollateralPosition

AryaFi.Finance.Collateral
Haircut & Margin

Collateral position tracking allocated assets against financing facilities. Manages haircut rates, daily mark-to-market revaluation, bilateral substitution, and default liquidation.

Signatories: collateralProvider, collateralReceiver, custodian
Formula: Value = marketValue × (1 − haircut)
Key Choices: RevalueCollateral, SubstituteCollateral, ReleaseCollateral
